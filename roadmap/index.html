Roadmap

From idea to every hospital.

Three phases over 24 months, and the milestones that get us there.

Phase 1Months 1–3

Pre-development

  • Assemble a core team of developers and designers
  • Train the team
  • Research user needs and the competition
  • Detailed plan with milestones
  • Secure funding and resources
Phase 2Months 4–18

Development

  • Build the core navigation app
  • Apply AI, cloud, and IoT to the experience
  • Rigorous testing for reliability and security
  • Improve continuously from user feedback
  • Integrate with partner hospitals
Phase 3Months 19–24

Launch and growth

  • Public launch of X-MED
  • Marketing campaign to drive adoption
  • Monitor feedback and iterate
  • Expand to healthcare institutions globally
  • Explore new technologies
